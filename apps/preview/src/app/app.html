03construction & clear space

One gap width everywhere. x = the signal panel’s vertical edge = ¼ of the mark height; keep at least 1x around mark and lockups. Use the favicon construction at 16 px, the small one at 20–40 px, the primary mark from 48 px.

04lockups

Wordmark: Inter Medium, lowercase, −0.015em, as outlines. Gap between mark and wordmark: 1x. Every lockup exists in dark, light, mono-white and mono-black, as SVG and PNG — see downloads.
